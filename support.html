Email support

Contact us

Email support@knowmypc.app. These help us answer in one reply:

  • The text from Settings → About → Copy support info (your version, Windows version and recent log lines; your license key isn't included)
  • What you expected, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot, if something looks wrong
  • For history questions, the file from Settings → History and data → Export JSON

Never send your full license key or any passwords. The first and last four characters of a key are enough to find your order.

Licenses and purchases

Where's my license key?

It's in the receipt email from Polar, sent right after you buy or subscribe. Check your spam folder. You can always find it again in the customer portal: enter the email you bought with.

Activating

  1. Open Know My PC and press Ctrl + , to open Settings.
  2. Go to License, paste your key and click Activate.

Activation needs an internet connection once. After that, Know My PC checks a lifetime key about once a week and a monthly key about once a day, and keeps working offline for a while (30 days for lifetime, 7 for monthly).

Monthly subscriptions

Your subscription renews every month. To change your card or cancel, open the customer portal and enter the email you subscribed with. When a subscription ends, fixes pause and Settings → License shows Subscription ended; seeing what's slowing your PC down still works. Renew it and Know My PC unlocks again by itself within a day, or right away with Check again. To switch to lifetime, buy it and paste the new key.

Moving to a new PC

Each key works on 2 PCs. On the old PC, open Settings → License → Move to another PC, then activate the key on the new one. If the old PC is gone, you can remove it in the customer portal.

Refunds and invoices

Email us within 14 days of buying lifetime, or of your first monthly payment, for a refund. Invoices and receipts are in the customer portal.

Installing and updating

“Windows protected your PC”

SmartScreen shows this for apps it hasn't seen many downloads of yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. Only run installers you downloaded from this site.

It says WebView2 is missing

Windows 11 includes it. On Windows 10, install the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, then run the installer again.

Updating to a new version

Know My PC checks for updates once a day and shows a notice when one is ready. Click Update now and it closes, updates and opens again by itself; your history, settings and license stay as they are. To check right away, open Settings → About → Check now. You can turn automatic checks off there too.

Using Know My PC

Windows asks for permission when I click something

Some changes need administrator rights: closing a game that runs as administrator, or changing a startup app for all users. Know My PC asks Windows, and Windows asks you. Click Yes to continue.

Undoing a change

Settings → Changes made lists everything Know My PC changed: apps it ended, startup apps it switched off, graphics settings and files it moved. Click Undo next to a startup or graphics change to put it back. Files it moved from your own folders are in the Recycle Bin, where you can restore them. Temp files and caches cleared with Free up space can't be undone.

A game won't close

Some anti-cheat systems block every other program from closing their game, even with administrator rights. Quit from inside the game or press Alt + F4.

History is empty or has gaps

History is recorded only while Know My PC is running. Turn on Settings → Window → Start with Windows, and set “When I close the window” to Keep watching.

I don't get alerts

Check that Overload alerts are on in Settings, and that Windows notifications are allowed for Know My PC (Windows Settings → System → Notifications). Alerts are held while a game is running unless you turn that off.

Uninstalling

Open Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find Know My PC and choose Uninstall. Your history and settings stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Know My PC in case you reinstall; delete that folder to remove them too. Deactivate your license first (Settings → License → Move to another PC) if you'll use it elsewhere.
